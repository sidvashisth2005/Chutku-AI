YOUR PHONE, ANYWHERE

Chutku on your phone.

Chat, talk, approve, watch the screen and (if you allow it here) control this laptop from your phone, through your own Tailscale network only. Every approval, screen view and control session asks for your phone PIN.

One button: it installs Tailscale if needed, opens its sign-in, turns phone access on and shows a code to scan.

Telegram

Send Chutku tasks from Telegram, approve them there, and get a "Laptop is ON" message after a restart.

Reach this laptop after a restart

After a restart or a power cut, Telegram gets a link that shows the Windows sign-in screen on your phone: tap, type your password, Sign in. Windows asks once for permission (Chutku itself never runs as administrator) and Fast Startup is turned off so that switching on is a real start. Needs the Telegram bot above and Tailscale (Connect my phone).

Direct control

On by default; each control session still asks for your phone PIN. Only this laptop turns it off (here, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P, or "remote band karo") and it stays off until you turn it on here. A red border shows while the phone controls the screen.
